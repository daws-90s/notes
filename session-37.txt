IAM -> Identity access management

User -> console access through username and password, keys for progrmmatic access
Group -> a group of users
Role -> non human users. services in AWS, users from other accounts, third party users called federated users can assume role. We can create role for ec2 service to provide access for creating other AWS services
Permissions -> What resources and actions
Policy  -> A list of permissions is called policy
Policy can be attached to user, group or role

Noun -> Resource
Verb -> Action on resource

EC2, S3, Cloudfront, LoadBalancer, etc.. -> Resources
Create EC2, Update EC2, ReadEC2, DeleteEC2 -> Actions on resources

Authentication -> Who you are -> can trust 3rd parties
Authorization -> What you are
